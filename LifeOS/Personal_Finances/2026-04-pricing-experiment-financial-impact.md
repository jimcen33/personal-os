# Pricing experiment — financial impact for me personally

## Context

Threadweaver currently at $19/mo (all tiers). Testing $29/mo for new signups starting 2026-04-22. Decision deadline 2026-05-30.

## Personal financial stakes

- TW MRR = $2.3K. My total monthly burn ~$3.4K (Lisbon rent, food, contractors, tooling, basic life).
- Difference covered by ~6 months of runway (~$22K savings).
- TW needs to clear $3.4K MRR to fund my full burn. ~52% growth from here.

## Pricing scenarios (rough math)

| Scenario | New MRR/customer | Conversion impact | Net 6-month effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stay at $19 | $19 | baseline | Status quo |
| Move to $29 (no conversion drop) | $29 | 0% | +$1,400/mo by month 6 (assuming 14 new/mo) |
| Move to $29 (-30% conversion) | $29 | -30% | +$300/mo by month 6 |
| Move to $29 (-50% conversion) | $29 | -50% | -$200/mo by month 6 |
| Move to $25 | $25 | -10% (guess) | +$700/mo by month 6 |

## Current data (4 weeks in)

- New trial signups: same as before (no top-of-funnel impact)
- Trial → paid: 22% (was 28% at $19) → ~21% drop in conversion
- Net MRR effect: +18% per new customer cohort

## Decision (pending)

Likely move to $25 (the unexplored middle option) rather than commit to $29 or revert to $19. Need to:
- Run 2 more weeks for sample size
- Talk to Ben on Thursday for outside view
- Check on existing-customer churn (might be price-anchored)

## Decision deadline

2026-05-30. If unresolved, default = revert to $19 (status quo).
