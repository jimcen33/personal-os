# Superhuman onboarding teardown (clipping)

Source: https://www.firstround.com/review/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit/

The "Sean Ellis" must-have-product survey turned into Superhuman's PMF compass. Key bits:

- Ask new users 4 weeks in: "How would you feel if you could no longer use [product]?"
- 40%+ "very disappointed" = PMF. Below 40% = not yet.
- Segment "very disappointed" responses. What's the common shape?
- Use to refine target persona, then double down.

Quoted from article:
> "We picked the segment of users who loved our product the most, profiled them, then went to work systematically attracting more people just like them."

Worth: medium-high if I can run it for TW. 140 customers - sample is small but enough. Need to think about Q3 ("scope-fit") at ingest time.
