# The atomic essay → product → audience flywheel

## Why ready for promote

4/4 gate. Methodology-shaped. Specific to my Wiki Scope. I've used this exact pattern across 4 newsletters and 2 product decisions. Hand-off to `/promote` in a fresh session.

## Pattern (drafted; Zone C will be added at promotion)

The flywheel:
1. Write an atomic essay (single argument, ~600 words, ships in a newsletter)
2. Extract the strongest claim. Build the smallest product feature that lets readers test that claim.
3. Ship the feature; the existing audience gives signal.
4. Write the next atomic essay from what you learned shipping.

Each loop: ~2 weeks. Newsletter readers become product testers. Product feedback becomes newsletter content.

## Examples from my own work

- Essay #2 ("Why I stopped optimizing for views") → product decision to remove the X-thread view-count display in the dashboard → essay #5 in the queue
- Essay #1 ("The 12-hour content engine") → product feature: scheduled batch processing → essay #6 in the queue (about scheduling)
