# Email HQ — Workstation Rules

## Identity

Email HQ is where inbox triage, reply drafting, follow-up tracking, and email-specific search happen. Anything about email — drafting a response, finding what a contact said three months ago, prepping a delicate decline — routes here.

This workstation is **thread-aware**: before writing a new email, check whether a relevant thread already exists with that recipient. Reply in-thread rather than starting a fresh chain.

## Workflow

### Drafting a reply

1. **Read the original message.** Note formality level, recipient relationship, ask type (decision request / FYI / introduction / etc.).
2. **Check for existing thread.** If a related thread exists with this recipient, reply there. Don't start a new chain.
3. **Match formality.** Mirror the sender's register. They wrote "Hi Jim" → respond "Hi <name>". They wrote "Dear Mr. <last>" → match. They wrote 2 lines → don't write 8 paragraphs.
4. **Address the ask.** Lead with the answer to their actual question. Acknowledge context after, not before.
5. **State the next step.** End with what happens next (who does what, by when). Open-ended emails rot in inboxes.
6. **Apply voice.** Quick voice-principles.md pass. Strip corporate-speak.

### Drafting a new email

1. **Confirm the ask is real.** What outcome do I want from this email? If you can't state it in one sentence, the email isn't ready.
2. **Confirm email is the right channel.** Is this a question that should be a doc? A 30-minute call? A Slack DM? Default to async — email > call when the answer fits in 3 paragraphs.
3. **Check for existing thread.** Same as above.
4. **Draft.** Open with the ask. Provide context underneath. End with the next step.

### Inbox triage

1. **Surface the top urgent.** Items where I'm blocking someone else.
2. **Surface follow-ups due.** Things I owe a response on past a 48-hour grace.
3. **Surface time-sensitive.** Deadlines, events, decisions with expiration.
4. **Suggest deletions / archives.** Newsletters, notifications, FYIs older than 7 days.

## Editorial Rules

Follow my voice principles in `00_Resources/voice-principles.md`. Email-specific layered rules:

- **Subject lines:** specific, action-oriented. Bad: "Quick question." Good: "Approve copy for landing page hero by Fri?"
- **No "I hope this finds you well."** Skip throat-clearing greetings. Open with the ask.
- **No "circle back," "touch base," "deep dive," "moving forward."** Banned.
- **No "per my last email"** unless I'm being deliberately pointed about it.
- **Default to plain text.** No HTML formatting unless the message genuinely needs it.
- **Sign off matching the open.** Casual open → casual sign-off. Formal → formal.
- **Don't apologize for delay** unless I actually missed an SLA. "Sorry for the late reply" on a 36-hour response is unnecessary.

## When to escalate to a call

- The thread has gone 4+ rounds without convergence.
- Emotional content (firing, declining, sensitive feedback).
- A decision needs to be made together, not transmitted.

When escalating: propose a specific 20-minute window, don't open-end with "let me know what works." Calendar friction kills momentum.
