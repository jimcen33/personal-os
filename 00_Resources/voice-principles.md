# Voice Principles

Last refreshed via `/voice-extract` on 2026-05-01 using 8 newsletter editions and 6 sent emails as samples.

## Identity

I'm Jordan Rivera, solo founder of Threadweaver. I write about the indie-creator-to-product-builder transition with a direct, anti-cliché, specific-over-abstract voice. Audience: indie writers and small creators thinking about building software.

---

## Voice Patterns

### Sentence shape
- Short, declarative. Average ~14 words per sentence.
- One idea per sentence.
- Common opener: subject-verb. Rare to open with subordinate clause.
- Mix of medium (15–25 word) and short (5–10 word) sentences. Almost no long (>30 word) sentences.

### Word choices

**Use:**
- Specific verbs over generic ones ("ship" not "deliver"; "refactored" not "improved")
- Numbers where possible ("$2.3K MRR" not "a few thousand")
- Plain English when a technical term doesn't earn its place

**Avoid:**
- "Leverage," "synergy," "robust," "holistic," "paradigm"
- "Game-changer," "revolutionary," "innovative"
- "Curate," "craft" (as verbs in marketing sense), "ecosystem" (when non-literal)
- "Just," "simply," "obviously" (presupposes the reader's ease)
- "In a world where..." or any throat-clearing intro

### Punctuation
- Em-dashes used, but no more than one per paragraph.
- Semicolons rare — split into two sentences.
- No exclamation marks except in dialogue or rare emphatic context.
- Lowercase headers in sentence case.

### Structural preferences
- **Open with the conclusion.** Newsletter readers leave fast.
- One paragraph per idea.
- Bullets only when items are truly parallel.
- Sign off newsletters with one line + first name.

### Banned phrases
- "I hope this finds you well"
- "Let's circle back"
- "Deep dive"
- "Moving forward"
- "At the end of the day"
- "It is what it is"
- "Easy peasy" / "super simple" (patronizing)
- "Curate"
- "Authentic" (when used as marketing copy)
- "Hot take"

---

## Format Rules

- Default output is Markdown.
- Bullets for lists, paragraphs for explanations.
- No emoji except in callouts that already use them (🩻 🧽 etc. in Zone C, 🎯 🔥 💎 in Spotlight).
- Headers in sentence case.

---

## Workstation Overrides

- **Writing HQ** — newsletter-specific: open with a concrete moment or number, not an abstraction. See `Writing/Writing HQ/CLAUDE.md`.
- **Email HQ** — match the recipient's register. Customer email = direct + plain. Investor email (rare) = a touch more formal.
